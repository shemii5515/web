About Us

MEA Engineering College (MEAEC), established in 2002, is a premier private, self-financing engineering institution located in Vengoor, Perinthalmanna,in the Malappuram district of Kerala. Governed by the Muslim Educational Association (MEA) and sponsored by the Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama, it offers quality technical education to students.
