FESTIVALS OF INDIA

India is a country with many colourful festivals.People celebrate festivals with joy and happiness.Each festivals has its own traditionals and customs.festivals bring people together and spread unity.

1.Diwali

Diwali is the festival of lights.It is celebrated with lamps,sweets,fireworks and decorations.

2.Onam

Onam is a famous festival of kerala.It is celebrated with flower decorations,traditional food and cultural events.

3.Holi

Holi is known as the festivals of colours poeple celebrate it by playing with colours and sharing sweets.
