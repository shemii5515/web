About Us

MEA Engineering College is a private engineering school in Nellikunnu, Vengoor, near Perinthalmanna, Malappuram district, Kerala. Started in 2002, it is run by the Muslim Educational Association and affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University. It holds NAAC accreditation and offers various undergraduate and postgraduate tech degrees.
